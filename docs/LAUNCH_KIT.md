# Profit-plan launch kit

This file turns the remaining owner-operated launch work into copy-ready
assets. It deliberately does not claim that external accounts, uploads, or
outreach have happened.

## Five-minute demo

1. Start LM Studio with a supported local model and launch L&D Command Center.
2. Open Language Lab → E.T.; type one short answer and show the correction,
   score, and saved conversation.
3. Generate a lesson slot for a real use case such as “customer-support call
   in Spanish”; open the interactive pack and click Share.
4. Open Career; search a listing, prepare an application package, and advance
   it in Campaign mode.
5. Close LM Studio, reopen the app, and show the cached identity, local pack,
   and offline license panel. End on: “Your tutor and career coach run on your
   laptop. Your documents and practice history stay there too.”

Keep the recording under five minutes. Use one screen capture, one narrator,
and no private resume or employer data.

## Outreach copy

### Language school

Subject: Private offline speaking practice for your students

I’m piloting L&D Command Center, a Windows app that gives students local
speaking practice, level exams, lesson packs, and spaced repetition without
sending student audio or learning history to a cloud AI service. I’m looking
for one 25-seat school pilot. I can set up a 30-day evaluation around one
language and collect feedback from teachers and students. Would a short demo
be useful next week?

### Outplacement or staffing firm

Subject: A private career campaign tool for job-seekers

L&D Command Center combines local resume tailoring, job search, application
packages, interview preparation, and follow-up tracking. It is designed for
clients whose career documents cannot be sent to a third-party AI service. I’m
offering a 25-seat pilot with a 30-day feedback loop. Could I show the full
resume-to-interview flow to the person who owns your candidate experience?

## Pilot acceptance checklist

- 25 machine-locked seats issued with `storage/licensing.py`
- one named pilot owner and one feedback channel
- baseline: applications prepared per participant in week one
- outcome: interviews or completed study sessions by day 30
- exit: convert, revise the product, or stop; record the decision and the
  measured numbers

External completion still requires the owner to create the checkout account,
upload the executable and demo, and send the messages. Those actions are not
safe to fabricate from the repository.
