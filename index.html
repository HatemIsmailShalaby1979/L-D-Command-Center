Three focus pillars

Language Lab + E.T.

The competitive wedge: a ready-made A1→C1 curriculum library in 15 everyday-life domains, live voice conversations with Mr. & Mrs. E.T. (mic in, accent + grammar evaluated, persona replies spoken aloud), inclusive level exams with full evaluations, bilingual packs with per-line audio, spaced repetition, deterministic graders. Nothing leaves your machine.

Career Development

The pricing power: upload resume, import GitHub, connect LinkedIn, enhance toward a role, watch job boards, prepare full application packages, draft LinkedIn posts. The whole career campaign loop, offline.

Skills Arena

The practice ground: import your own documents for graded reading packs, write for rubric evaluation with corrected versions, import audio for transcript + comprehension questions, mock full interviews with E.T. Media workspace + connectors stay.
